product / maintainer controls

keeping humans in control

Maintainer Controls

01

Collaborators only

Shipped, June 2026

Restrict who can open issues to people with write access. Every public entry point closes with it: issues, comments, Discussions, Projects and Copilot.

02

Agent automation controls

Public preview, July 2026

Agents suggest instead of apply. Labels, types, assignments and closures wait for approval, auto-apply above a confidence threshold you set, and record a rationale for every action.

03

Triage role exceptions

Shipped, August 2026

Delegate without reopening the door. Trusted triagers keep filing and sorting issues while the restriction stays on for everyone else.

Gate the front door. Delegate safely. Keep agents on a leash inside the issue.
Source: GitHub Changelog, June to August 2026.
